Flight Info Source

"Local Timing" derives status purely from the published schedule (the app's original behavior). "AeroAPI" polls FlightAware AeroAPI for the current and next flight on a dynamic schedule, staged coarser-to-finer as each moment approaches -- every 1 minute through gate-out/wheels-up/wheels-down/gate-in transitions (and again in the last 15 minutes before departure / 10 before touchdown), every 5 minutes for the last 2 hours airborne before that, every 15 minutes for the rest of the flight (and for the last 6 hours before departure), every 60 minutes for the rest of the 24-hour pre-departure window, and nothing at all before that (caps the AeroAPI query cost at a fixed amount per flight, no matter how long it sits on the board ahead of time) -- and shows actual/estimated times, gate, and a live status once available, falling back to the published time until AeroAPI reports something newer. This only ever applies to Real World Data; Simulated Data always shows locally-derived timing.
